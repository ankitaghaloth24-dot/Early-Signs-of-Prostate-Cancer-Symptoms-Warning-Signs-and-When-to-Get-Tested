# Early-Signs-of-Prostate-Cancer-Symptoms-Warning-Signs-and-When-to-Get-Tested
Recognising the early signs of prostate cancer and understanding personal risk factors can help men make informed decisions about their health. Although early-stage disease often causes no symptoms, changes in urination, blood in the urine or semen and persistent discomfort should be evaluated by a medical professional.
